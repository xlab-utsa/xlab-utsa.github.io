Dr. Jinjun Xiong has begun his role as founding dean of UT San Antonio's College of AI, Cyber and Computing, effective March 1, 2026. The college officially launched September 1, 2025, and Dr. Xiong is its first dean.

He joins UTSA from the University at Buffalo, where he was the SUNY Empire Innovation Professor and director of the Institute for Artificial Intelligence and Data Science; he remains an adjunct professor there. He continues to direct the $20M National AI Institute for Exceptional Education and lead the AI Thrust for the National Center for Early Literacy and Responsible AI.
